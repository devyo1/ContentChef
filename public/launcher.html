First time? Start here.Your first brand and ad, step by step. No design experience needed.+

  1. Open your studio

    Click Open studio above. The studio is where you make your ads. Example brands are there to try; you can make your own next.

  2. Connect your assistant once

    Click Settings at the top, then find AI assistant. Under Connect with, choose My ChatGPT account (via Codex). Click Sign in with ChatGPT, finish in the browser, then return and click Check again. If it already says Signed in with ChatGPT, you’re ready.

    If you see Codex not found, use the setup help in the full guide below. You can also choose your Claude account here.

  3. Give us your website and idea

    Click the brand name near the top left, then New brand… → From my website. Enter your website address and describe what you sell and who you want to reach. Click Build my brand and wait for the suggestions.

    Promote my photography services to local business owners. Make the brand feel friendly, modern and professional.

    Check the name, colours and words, then click Create brand & fine-tune. You can change them later. No website? Choose Start manually.

  4. Ask for your first ad

    Click Ask ChatGPT (or Ask Claude) at the top. Type something like this and press Enter:

    Make one Instagram feed ad for my brand, promoting our services to local business owners. Use a friendly headline and a “Learn more” button. Show it in the editor so I can review it.

    Wait for the design to appear. Want a change? Try “Make the headline shorter” or “Use a lighter background.”

  5. Save a picture you can post

    In Create, find Export, choose PNG (best) and click Export … file(s). When it says Served!, click Open folder to find your pictures. Upload a picture to your social account when you’re ready; Content Chef doesn’t post it for you.
