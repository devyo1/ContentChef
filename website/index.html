AI at the pass

Claude or ChatGPT cooks with you, in your builder.

  • You watch it work. Ask for an ad and it appears in your editor: copy, versions, templates and colours, laid out side by side.
  • Nothing gets rendered behind your back. Files are only made when you say export.
  • It writes in your voice. One click for headlines, body copy, bulk lists and captions for every platform.
  • Use the account you already have. Sign in with Claude or ChatGPT; no API key needed. Works as an MCP connector too.

How a post gets made

Four stations, one pass.

Words

Type a headline or let your assistant write it. Add versions to test, or paste a whole spreadsheet for bulk ads.

Look

Pick templates and colourways from your brand kit, add a photo or video, crop it, drop on a sticker.

Motion

Typewriter, word rise, holo shimmer, a shining button. Loops snap to lengths that repeat perfectly.

Sizes

Tick the platforms. Every size renders at once and lands in one folder with its captions.
